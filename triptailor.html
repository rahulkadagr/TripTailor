Frequently Asked Questions

How does TripTailor work?

You share your destination, dates, budget, and preferences. We design an itinerary with suitable options, you review and adjust it, and then you confirm the arrangements you want.

Can I customize my itinerary?

Yes. Hotels, transport, activities, and budget can all be adjusted to your needs.

Do I need to book the entire package through TripTailor?

No. You can select only the services you need rather than a rigid package. Exact arrangements will be confirmed before launch.

How much does trip planning cost?

Illustrative planning fees start at ₹299 (Tailor Basic), ₹699 (Tailor Plus), and from ₹1,499 (Tailor Premium). Fees are separate from travel bookings, and the final scope and fee are confirmed before purchase.

Can you plan trips within a fixed budget?

Yes, that's a core idea. Share your per-person budget and we'll shape options around it. We can't promise lowest prices.

Can I modify my itinerary?

Yes. The number of revisions depends on the plan you choose.

Are the prices shown on the website final?

No. All prices are illustrative starting estimates, not live quotes. Final pricing depends on dates, traveler count, hotel category, transport, availability, and activities.

What happens if a booking is cancelled?

Cancellation terms depend on the specific booking and the supplier's conditions. Our cancellation policy is still a draft and will be confirmed before launch.

How do I contact TripTailor during my trip?

You'll have a single point of contact via WhatsApp, phone, or email. Exact support arrangements will be confirmed before launch.
